# 60-Second Client Explanation

Use this when a client asks, on a call, "so what is this project?" It's written to be
**spoken**, not read. Roughly 150 words / ~60 seconds at a normal pace.

---

> "So this is a demo I built to show how I take messy documents and turn them into clean,
> reliable data automatically.
>
> Picture the work your team does today: a project document comes in — a material
> schedule, a scope note, a scanned PDF — and someone has to read it and retype the
> important fields into a spreadsheet. Project number, materials, quantities, units,
> deadlines. It's slow, and it's where mistakes sneak in.
>
> My system does that read-and-retype step automatically. But the important part is the
> second step: it then *checks* everything it pulled out against your own reference
> data and rules. So if a quantity is negative, or a material code doesn't exist, or a
> unit is wrong, it catches it and flags that document for a quick human look — instead
> of letting a bad number land silently in your spreadsheet.
>
> The clean documents just flow through; your team only spends time on the exceptions.
> I built it to be honest about that boundary — automate the routine, escalate the
> uncertain."

---

### If they ask "is this from a real client?"

> "No — I built it myself as a demonstration, so there's no client data or savings
> numbers attached to it. The code is real and runs; I'd adapt the validation rules and
> reference data to your actual documents."

### One-sentence version (elevator)

> "It reads messy project documents, pulls out the key data automatically, and — the
> part that matters — validates every field against your own rules so bad data gets
> flagged before it reaches your spreadsheet."
